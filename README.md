## 🚀 Engineering Architecture & Focus

- 🗄️ **Petabyte-Scale Storage:** Deploying and maintaining distributed object and block storage clusters with **Ceph** and **SeaweedFS**, ensuring high availability and zero-loss failover.
- ⚡ **High-Concurrency Systems:** Engineering low-latency Layer-7 load balancers, caching engines, and networking microservices in **Go** utilizing goroutines and channels.
- 📊 **Telemetry & Observability:** Building automated telemetry pipelines with **Prometheus**, **Grafana**, and the **ELK Stack (Elasticsearch, Logstash, Kibana)** for microservice tracing and monitoring.
- 🤖 **Resilient Automation:** Designing containerized, high-throughput workflows with **n8n (Queue Mode)** backed by Redis and PostgreSQL.
